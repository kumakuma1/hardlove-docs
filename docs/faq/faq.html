FAQ

Answers to the questions that come up most often. Click a question to open it.

What emulator should I use?
I recommend melonDS. DeSmuME and DraStic (on Android) also work well.
Can I play the game on hardware?
I don't know, and I have no way of testing it.
Is Hardlove Gold built with Emerald-Expansion or CFRU?
No. It is built with hg-engine.
Is Hardlove Gold harder than Null, Run&Bun or Platinum Kaizo?
I don't think it's harder overall. However, the endgame is more unfair than Run&Bun's.
Is Hardlove Gold just Run&Bun in Gen 4?
It definitely leans in that direction, but hopefully it brings something new to the table and mixes things up enough to stand on its own.
Why was Hardlove so heavily influenced by Run&Bun?
In my opinion, Run&Bun created an entirely new genre of difficulty hacks with the balancing, restrictions and mechanics it introduced. I really liked them. Kudos to the creator.
Does Dekzeh know you "stole" so much from Run&Bun?
No. I reached out to him but never got an answer.
[insert name] is also in Platinum Kaizo. Did you copy it from there?
No. I knew nothing about Platinum Kaizo before its release, and I don't know its creators either.
Who created the AI for Hardlove Gold?
I did. I wrote it from scratch with some help from Run&Bun's spec document. I also asked Rain and Terra for their opinions on certain topics.
Does the AI cheat or read player inputs?
No.
Why does the AI calculate switch-in scores against both Pokémon in double battles?
This makes the AI play very aggressively or try to pivot into more stable positions before sending out frail sweepers later in the fight.
Why did the AI use [insert name] in situation [insert description]?
I have no idea. I would need a save file to debug it, or the debug output from that situation.
Some fights seem unfair and unbalanced? Why?
Yes.
Why do I have to fight every trainer? Why are there so many RNG moments?
I see Pokémon as a gigantic slot machine. There are enough unfair and RNG moments to whittle down your team and box, so that the boss fights can finish you off.
Why doesn't [insert Pokémon] have [insert move or ability]?
hg-engine, in its current state, still has a few unimplemented moves, abilities and mechanics.
Do I have to play it as a Nuzlocke?
No. Although the game is designed for Nuzlockes, you are welcome to play it however you want.
The game is too hard for me. How can I play it anyway?
There is a built-in easy mode you can choose at the start of the game during Mom's dialogue. (I'd appreciate any feedback on it to make it a better casual playthrough.)
I got a Rayquaza. Was this intended?
No. I added it so I could speedrun the game while debugging a specific situation, and I never removed it.
Why is the rest of Kanto missing from the game?
I want the adventure to take place mainly in Johto. Including Kanto would have made the game bloated and way too long.
Did you beat Run&Bun/[insert name] as a Nuzlocke?
No. I am terrible at playing Pokémon.
Is Pokémon [insert name] in the game?
There is a comprehensive list of unobtainable Pokémon in the encounter documentation.
Why is Pokémon [insert name] not obtainable?
Simply because I decided so.
Why are so few Gen 9 Pokémon obtainable?
I don't like them that much.
Which Pokémon were changed or buffed?
There are no type or stat changes. A few Pokémon had their abilities changed for balancing purposes.
Does the game have custom maps?
No, only minor functional changes to a few maps.
Why do some Pokémon show up as Bulbasaur in battle?
Bulbasaur is hg-engine's fallback sprite for Pokémon whose sprites aren't finished yet. It's just a placeholder.
How long did it take to make the game?
It took roughly 3 years, although most of that time was spent modernizing hg-engine.
How did you manage to work on this one thing for so long?
I spent almost 10 years working in software engineering. I sat through countless unnecessary meetings, warned managers about stupid decisions, watched those warnings get ignored, and then got tasked with fixing the exact problems I had warned them about. That builds frustration tolerance. (But hey, it was fun too, sometimes.)
Who helped you build the game?
A few awesome people helped with minor map edits, playtesting and team balancing. I also owe a massive thank-you to the many contributors to hg-engine! If you want to help or give feedback going forward, I'd really appreciate it.
How can I reach out to you if I want to help?
Just join the Hardlove Gold Discord.
I want to make a game like this. How do I start?
Learn to program (a bit). Seriously. hg-engine is not beginner-friendly compared to Gen 3 hacking tools. That said, Gen 4 also has some easier hacking tools that require less coding.
I encountered a bug. How do I report it?
Ideally, open a thread on the Hardlove Gold Discord. General battle engine bugs can also be reported on the hg-engine GitHub repo.
Do you stream your playthroughs?
No, I am not a streamer.
Are you planning to make more ROM hacks?
No.
Is there anything else I should know before I start playing?
Just dive in and consult the documentation if you need to. Enjoy!
